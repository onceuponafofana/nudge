What Nudge is

Nudge is a consumer app that records audio while you sleep, estimates when snoring occurred, and can produce a vibration intended to prompt a change in sleeping position. It also generates masking sound.

Nudge is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition, and nothing it shows you should be used in place of advice from a qualified clinician.

Health and safety

Snoring accompanied by gasping, choking, or pauses in breathing may indicate obstructive sleep apnea, a condition that requires medical assessment. Do not use Nudge to decide whether you need care.

Suggestions shown in the app are general information, not personal advice. Some, such as mouth taping, are not safe for everyone and should not be used by anyone with untreated sleep apnea or nasal obstruction.

Do not place a phone under a pillow or under bedding. A charging device covered by insulating material can overheat.

Accuracy

Snoring detection is an estimate produced by software listening through a phone microphone. It can miss snoring and it can occasionally register something else as snoring. Figures shown in the app describe what the app detected, not a clinical measurement.
