What it does

Three things, about snoring.

Track it

Minutes snored, longest unbroken stretch, and the loudest twelve seconds, playable in the morning.

Interrupt it

A tap when snoring goes on too long. Most snoring stops when position changes. An Apple Watch works best, since it is already touching you. Without one, the phone can vibrate lying flat on the mattress.

Block it

Sound shaped to the frequencies snoring occupies, for any night you are the one being kept awake.

Better in pairs

Nudge works alone. It works better with someone.

One phone hears the snoring. The other plays the sound that covers it. No single phone can do both, because anything playing sound cannot hear over itself.

Link two phones and the sound follows the snoring as it happens, rising when it starts and settling when it stops. Either of you can unlink at any point, and neither ever sees the other's recordings.

Why the sound is different

Most sound machines are the wrong shape for snoring.

Snoring is a low sound. Palatal snoring, the common kind, sits roughly between 21 and 323 Hz with a peak near 137 Hz, and masking works upward from a frequency far better than downward.

White noise puts about nine tenths of its energy above 2 kHz, which is nowhere near snoring and is the part that sounds harsh. Nudge generates sound with most of its energy sitting directly on the snoring band instead, and it drifts slowly rather than sitting still, so it reads as weather rather than as a machine.

Honestly

It measures whether it worked.

When snoring qualifies for a nudge, Nudge flips a coin. Heads it nudges, tails it deliberately stays quiet and keeps watching. Both outcomes are recorded the same way.

That gives every night its own control group, from the same person in the same room, which is the only way to find out whether a tap on the wrist actually shortens snoring rather than assuming it does. No other snoring app checks.

Privacy

It stays on the phone.

Audio is recorded and analysed on your device. Nudge has no servers and no account, so there is nowhere for it to be sent. Recordings are trimmed after a few nights and deleted after that unless you keep one. Apple Health is read only, never written to.

Questions

Common questions

Will the nudge wake me up?

Usually not, and that is the design. The threshold for noticing something rises while you are asleep, so a tap can be strong enough to make you shift position while staying below the level that wakes you.

This is how clinical positional therapy works. In a randomised trial of 101 people, a vibrating position trainer cut apnea events from 18 an hour to 10, and at twelve months it performed as well as a dental appliance. Nudge starts at the smallest tap it has and only steps up if a smaller one is not landing.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

Only for the nudge itself. Without one, Nudge still records the night, measures how long you snored, tracks your trends and tells you which of your own habits make it worse. The phone can also vibrate, lying flat on the mattress so it is felt rather than heard.

Does it work if I have a sound machine?

Yes. Nudge uses Apple's sound recogniser rather than loudness alone, so a fan or a sound machine is not mistaken for snoring.

Why does the app have to stay on screen?

iPhone stops apps using the microphone once they are not in the foreground. Nudge keeps the screen awake and fades it to near black, which costs very little on an OLED display, and the phone should be charging anyway.

How much space do the recordings take?

Audio is about 15 MB an hour. Nights are kept whole for two days, then trimmed to just the snoring, then deleted after a few days unless you pin one. Reports and trends are kilobytes and are kept indefinitely.

Is this a medical device?

No. Nudge records what happens and does not diagnose anything. Loud snoring with gasping, choking or pauses in breathing can be sleep apnea, and only a sleep study can tell you. If any of that sounds familiar it is worth raising with a doctor.
