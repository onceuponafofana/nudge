Nudge

Privacy policy

Nudge does not collect anything. There is no account, no server, and nothing to upload to.

Last updated 29 August 2026.

What Nudge records

Nudge records audio through your device's microphone while a night is running, and analyses it on the device to work out when snoring occurred. Recordings, the reports made from them, and everything else the app produces are stored only in the app's own storage on your iPhone.

Recordings are kept whole for a short period, then trimmed to only the stretches containing snoring, then deleted entirely after a few nights unless you choose to keep a specific night. You can delete all recordings at any time from Settings.

What is never transmitted

Nudge operates no servers and has no backend. Audio, reports, sleep data, check-in answers and settings are never sent anywhere. The developer cannot access them and neither can anyone else.

Nudge contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking identifiers and no third party SDKs.

Apple Health

If you grant access, Nudge reads sleep analysis, blood oxygen, respiratory rate, resting heart rate and breathing disturbance data from Apple Health so it can display them alongside your snoring. This data is read on the device and is never transmitted.

Nudge never writes to Apple Health. You may revoke access at any time in the Health app, and Nudge will continue to work without it.

Apple Watch

If you use the Apple Watch app, it communicates with your iPhone over Apple's WatchConnectivity framework. The messages carry only a haptic strength and a battery percentage. No audio is ever sent to the watch.

If you connect with another person

A future version will let two people link their phones so masking sound can respond to snoring detected on the other device. If you use that feature, the only thing shared is a measure of how much snoring is happening. Recordings are never shared with a linked person, and either person can unlink at any time.

This feature does not exist at the time of writing. This policy will be updated before it does.

Purchases

Subscriptions, when available, are handled entirely by Apple. Nudge never sees your payment details.

Children

Nudge is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. Since it collects nothing from anyone, there is nothing held about any user of any age.

Your control

Deleting the app removes every recording, report and setting from your device. There is no copy held anywhere else, so there is nothing to request, export or ask to have erased.

Not medical advice

Nudge records what happens during the night. It does not diagnose any condition. Loud snoring with gasping, choking or pauses in breathing can indicate sleep apnea, which only a clinician can assess.
